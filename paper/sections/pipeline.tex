\subsection{Filter bands}
\label{subsec:filter_bands}

\subsection{Feature extraction}
\label{subsec:features_extract}

Features are computed from the cleaned voltage signal for each
sweep-sensor pair for each sub-band. In total 26 features are computed, comprising 12 time-domain and 14 frequency-domain statistics,
as listed in Tables~\ref{tab:features} and~\ref{tab:features_freq}. All features (without the specific sub-bands) have previously been applied to signals for bearing condition monitoring and LCM~\cite{jakobsen_detecting_2023,zhang_graph_2024}; the Hjorth parameters originate from EEG analysis~\cite{hjorth_eeg_1970}.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\caption{Time-domain features. Notation: $N$ signal length; $\bar{x}$ sample mean;
  $\sigma$ population standard deviation.}
\label{tab:features}
\centering
{\small\setstretch{1}%
\begin{tabular}{@{} p{2.8cm} p{6.2cm} p{6.2cm} @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Name} & \textbf{Formula} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
Peak & $(\max x - \min x)/2$ & Half the peak-to-peak range. \\[3pt]
RMS & $\sqrt{N^{-1}\sum_{n}x_n^2}$ & Root mean square amplitude. \\[3pt]
Std & $\sqrt{N^{-1}\sum_{n}(x_n-\bar{x})^2}$ & Population standard deviation. \\[3pt]
Variance & $N^{-1}\sum_{n}(x_n-\bar{x})^2$ & Population variance (square of Std). \\[3pt]
Skewness & $N^{-1}\sum_{n}[(x_n-\bar{x})/\sigma]^3$ & Third standardised central moment; amplitude asymmetry. \\[3pt]
Kurtosis & $N^{-1}\sum_{n}[(x_n-\bar{x})/\sigma]^4$ & Fourth standardised central moment; tail heaviness. \\[3pt]
Crest factor & $x_{\text{peak}}/x_{\text{RMS}}$ & Peak-to-RMS ratio; sensitive to impulsive events. \\[3pt]
Shape factor & $x_{\text{RMS}}\,/\,(N^{-1}\sum_n|x_n|)$ & Ratio of RMS to mean absolute value. \\[3pt]
Impulse factor & $x_{\text{peak}}\,/\,(N^{-1}\sum_n|x_n|)$ & Ratio of peak to mean absolute value. \\[3pt]
Margin factor & $x_{\text{peak}}\,/\,(N^{-1}\sum_n\sqrt{|x_n|})^2$ & Peak divided by squared mean square-root amplitude. \\[3pt]
Hjorth mobility & $[\operatorname{Var}(x')/\operatorname{Var}(x)]^{1/2}$ & Square root of first-derivative to signal variance ratio; proportional to mean frequency. \\[3pt]
Hjorth complexity & $M(x')/M(x)$ & Ratio of first-derivative mobility $M$ to signal mobility; bandwidth proxy. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}
\end{table}

All frequency-domain features are computed from the one-sided FFT magnitude spectrum of
the cleaned signal.

\textbf{AE sub-band decomposition.}
In addition to broadband features, three frequency sub-bands are carved from the AE
channel using zero-phase Butterworth bandpass filters:
(i)~20--500~kHz, capturing the transition from audible to ultrasonic contact dynamics;
(ii)~500~kHz--1~MHz, the primary stress-wave propagation band; and
(iii)~1--2~MHz, covering high-frequency micro-fracture events.
The full 26-feature set is extracted independently for each sub-band, yielding
$26 \times 4 = 104$~candidate AE features (broadband plus three sub-bands).

\textbf{US channel pre-filtering and sub-band decomposition.}
The US channel is pre-filtered to 0--20~kHz by a zero-phase low-pass Butterworth filter
before feature extraction, consistent with the effective bandwidth of the heterodyned probe.
Two sub-bands are additionally extracted: 0--10~kHz and 10--20~kHz.
The broadband plus two sub-band sets yield $26 \times 3 = 78$~candidate US features.
