\section{Per-feature operating-condition correlations}
\label{app:feature_oc}
%% ============================================================================

Table~\ref{tab:feature_oc} lists the signed Spearman $\rho$ between each
candidate feature and the measured rotational speed (RPM) and housing
temperature, both marginally (over all segments) and conditionally
(partition-based: within-RPM-step for the temperature axis;
within-temperature-block for the speed axis). The conditional values are
medians across all valid partitions containing at least 30 segments and
8 distinct levels of the target variable. Features are grouped by sensor and
frequency sub-band. This table supports the discussion in
Section~\ref{subsec:confound}.
